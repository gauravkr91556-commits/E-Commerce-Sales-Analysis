# E-Commerce-Sales-Analysis
# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 1 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing **E-Commerce sales and profit data** to understand business performance and identify important sales and profit trends.

The analysis covers monthly sales, product categories, sub-categories, customer segments, and the relationship between sales and profit.

## 2 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

1. Analyse **monthly sales** and identify the month with the highest and lowest sales.
2. Analyse sales by **product category** and identify the highest and lowest-selling categories.
3. Analyse sales performance by **sub-category**.
4. Analyse **monthly profit** and identify the month with the highest profit.
5. Analyse profit by **category and sub-category**.
6. Analyse **sales and profit by customer segment**.
7. Calculate and analyse the **Sales-to-Profit Ratio**.

## 3 Key Analysis

### Monthly Sales Analysis

Compare sales across different months to understand seasonal trends and identify the highest and lowest sales months.

### Category Analysis

Analyse sales performance across different product categories and identify which categories contribute the most and least to sales.

### Sub-Category Analysis

Compare sales and profit across different product sub-categories to understand product-level performance.

### Monthly Profit Analysis

Analyse monthly profit and identify the month with the highest profit.

### Category & Sub-Category Profit Analysis

Understand which categories and sub-categories generate higher or lower profits.

### Customer Segment Analysis

Compare sales and profit across different customer segments to understand customer contribution to the business.

### Sales-to-Profit Ratio

Calculate the relationship between sales and profit to understand how effectively sales are converted into profit.
